Purpose

I started 'Andhe Ki Lathi' with a single question in mind — what if technology could be the eyes for those who cannot see? As a developer and problem solver, I saw an opportunity to combine AI, computer vision, and affordable hardware into something truly meaningful.


This isn’t just a product. It’s a mission. A movement. A tribute to every person who navigates life in darkness, and a promise that no one should have to walk alone. ‘Andhe Ki Lathi’ is their silent companion — always aware, always ready.
